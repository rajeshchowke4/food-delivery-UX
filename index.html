How to use in Figma

⚡ Method 1: Instant Copy & Paste (Easiest)

Click 📋 Copy Full Board to Figma above, then switch to Figma and press Ctrl + V (or Cmd + V). Figma natively converts the SVG into vector layers and frames!

📁 Method 2: Drag & Drop

Drag the food_delivery_ux_board.svg file or any screen from the screens/ folder directly into your Figma canvas.

🪄 Method 3: Figma Plugin Script

Open figma_generator_script.js in this folder, copy its code, open Figma Console (under Plugins > Development > Open Console) and press Enter to generate native frames.
