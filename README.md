# Food Delivery UX Planning Board & Wireframes (Figma-Ready)

This repository contains the exact replica of the **Mobile UX Planning Board: "Dinner, without the guesswork"** and all **8 End-to-End Mobile Wireframe Screens**, structured specifically for **Figma**.

---

## 🚀 How to Open & Edit in Figma (3 Easy Ways)

### Option 1: Drag & Drop (Fastest & 100% Native)
1. Open **Figma** in your browser or desktop app and create a new design file.
2. Drag and drop `food_delivery_ux_board.svg` directly from this folder onto your Figma canvas.
3. Figma will immediately convert every element into **native, fully-editable Figma layers, vector shapes, groups, and text objects**!

---

### Option 2: Individual Mobile Wireframe Screens (Standard 390x844)
If you want to prototype or inspect individual screens:
- Navigate to the `screens/` folder.
- Drag any of the 8 screen SVGs into Figma:
  - `screens/01_discover.svg` (Screen 01: Discover)
  - `screens/02_compare.svg` (Screen 02: Compare)
  - `screens/03_choose.svg` (Screen 03: Choose)
  - `screens/04_customize.svg` (Screen 04: Customize)
  - `screens/05_review.svg` (Screen 05: Review)
  - `screens/06_checkout.svg` (Screen 06: Checkout)
  - `screens/07_confirm.svg` (Screen 07: Confirm)
  - `screens/08_track.svg` (Screen 08: Track)
- Each screen is sized to standard mobile device dimensions (390 x 844 px) with status bar, dynamic island, and UI components ready for Figma prototyping connections!

---

### Option 3: Interactive Web Hub (One-Click Copy to Figma)
1. Double-click `index.html` to open it in your browser (Chrome/Edge).
2. Click **"📋 Copy Full Board to Figma"** (or click "Copy" on any specific screen).
3. Switch over to your Figma canvas and press **`Ctrl + V`** (or `Cmd + V`).
4. It will paste instantly as editable vectors and text!

---

### Option 4: Figma Console Automation Script
If you want to generate native Figma Auto Layout frames via code:
1. Open Figma, go to `Plugins` > `Development` > `Open Console`.
2. Open `figma_generator_script.js`, copy the code, and paste it into the Figma Console.
3. Press Enter. Figma will programmatically generate the board on your active canvas.

---

## 📋 Board Structure & Content Included
- **Header**: Sprint badge, "Dinner, without the guesswork", audience & platform tags, Design Challenge card with constraints.
- **01 · Research Foundations**: Assumptions, User Needs, Pain Points, Key Focuses.
- **02 · Two People to Design With**: Maya Patel (Busy renter) and Daniel Ruiz (Parent of two) with goals, behaviors, and frictions.
- **03 · Primary User Flow**: 8-step journey from Discover to Track with recovery rules.
- **04 · Lo-Fi Wireframe Flow (v0.1)**: 8 phone mockups with all UI states, sticky CTAs, itemized prices, delivery milestones, and map view.
- **05 · Design Thinking Record**: Empathize, Define, Ideate, Prototype (CURRENT), Test, plus Problem Framing, Major Decisions, and Next Validation Steps.
