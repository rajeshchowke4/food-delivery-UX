# 🍔 Food Delivery Mobile App — UX/UI Design

A mobile food-delivery app UX/UI design focused on making restaurant discovery, food customization, checkout, and order tracking simple and predictable.

The project explores the complete user journey from **discovering a restaurant to receiving an order**, with an emphasis on reducing uncertainty and helping users make confident decisions.

---

## 📱 Project Overview

The design concept focuses on a common food-delivery problem:

> **How can ordering food feel fast and easy without hiding the details people need to trust their decision?**

The solution uses a clear, step-by-step ordering experience where users can:

- Discover restaurants
- Search for specific restaurants or dishes
- Compare restaurant options
- View restaurant and menu details
- Customize food orders
- Review their order
- Complete checkout
- Receive order confirmation
- Track delivery progress

---

## 🎯 Design Goal

The primary goal was to design an end-to-end food-ordering experience that provides users with the right information at each stage of the journey.

### Key Design Principles

- **Clarity** — Present important information without overwhelming the user.
- **Transparency** — Clearly show prices, delivery information, and order details.
- **Confidence** — Help users understand what they are ordering before checkout.
- **Simple Navigation** — Keep the ordering journey easy to follow.
- **Progress Visibility** — Allow users to understand the current status of their order.
